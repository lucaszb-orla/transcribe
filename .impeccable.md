## Design Context

### Users
Professionals who use Transcribe immediately before and during meetings on a Mac. They need to grant sensitive system access quickly, understand why each permission exists, and trust that audio and transcripts stay on their device.

### Brand Personality
Private, energetic, and precise. The interface should feel confidently native to macOS while giving Transcribe one memorable editorial signature instead of looking like a generic utility.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, high-contrast editorial composition with chrome as a focused material accent. The existing chrome quotation artwork is the visual anchor; warm and cyan reflections may appear sparingly inside the metal treatment. Avoid rainbow gradients, persistent neon, glass-heavy cards, hidden native window chrome, and decorative motion outside onboarding.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with local-first trust: “100% no seu Mac” is the central promise and permission copy explains concrete benefits.
2. Make one moment memorable: reserve the living chrome frame and hero artwork for onboarding.
3. Keep the path obvious: present one essential permission at a time, then offer Calendar as a clearly optional extra.
4. Preserve platform integrity: use native window controls, keyboard behavior, system permission prompts, and resizable layouts.
5. Motion communicates progress: coordinate the border hotspot, number, and symbol, while providing static or opacity-only alternatives for reduced motion.
6. Accessibility is structural: maintain strong contrast, meaningful VoiceOver values, decorative artwork semantics, and readable layouts at minimum size.
